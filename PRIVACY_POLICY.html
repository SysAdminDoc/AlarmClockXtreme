AlarmClockXtreme Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 11, 2026

Overview

AlarmClockXtreme is an open-source alarm clock application. The developer does not operate a backend service for this app, does not receive your alarm data, and does not collect analytics, advertising, or account data. Some optional features make direct network requests from your device to third-party services or to endpoints you configure.

Data Collection

AlarmClockXtreme does not collect personal information for the developer. Specifically:

  • No analytics or tracking SDKs are included
  • No advertising frameworks are included
  • No user accounts or registration are required
  • No crash reports are sent automatically; crash logs are stored locally on your device only
  • No developer-operated server receives your alarms, settings, calendar events, health data, crash logs, downloads, or backup files

Permissions

The app requests permissions only for features you use:

  • Exact Alarms (USE_EXACT_ALARM) — Required to fire alarms at the exact time you set
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS and POST_PROMOTED_NOTIFICATIONS) — To show alarm alerts, promoted alarm updates where supported, and the persistent next-alarm notification
  • Foreground Service — To play alarm sounds, monitor smart-alarm windows, and run configured alarm-related work reliably
  • Full-Screen Intent — To show the alarm screen when an alarm fires
  • Boot Completed — To reschedule your alarms after device restart
  • Wake Lock — To keep the screen on while an alarm is firing
  • Vibrate — To vibrate the device when an alarm fires
  • Notification Policy Access — Optional. Used only if you enable the bedtime Do Not Disturb rule
  • Battery Optimization Exemption — Optional. Used only when you choose to whitelist the app for more reliable alarms on aggressive Android builds.
  • Location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION and ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION) — Optional. Coarse location is used for local weather, radar centering, and weather alerts. Fine location is requested by Android for Wi-Fi challenge matching on newer devices. Coordinates are sent directly to selected weather services only for weather features. You can use manual location instead.
  • Calendar (READ_CALENDAR) — Optional. Used only to display today's events on the dashboard. Calendar data is read locally and never transmitted.
  • NFC — Optional. Used for the NFC dismiss challenge.
  • Camera — Optional. Used for barcode/QR and photo-match dismiss challenges.
  • Microphone — Optional. Used for experimental sonar sleep tracking when enabled.
  • Health Connect sleep permission (android.permission.health.READ_SLEEP) — Optional and Play flavor only. Used only after you opt in and grant Health Connect access so Bedtime and Statistics can read recent sleep-session summaries.
  • Activity Recognition — Optional. Used for step and smart-alarm features.
  • SMS and Phone — Optional. Used only for the Guardian Angel emergency contact feature.
  • Audio and Storage — Optional. Used to browse audio files, control alarm audio behavior, and save downloaded alarm sounds to device media storage on supported Android versions.

Network Requests

The app can make the following network requests depending on enabled features. Requests are made directly from your device; the developer does not proxy or receive them.

  • Open-Meteo Weather API (api.open-meteo.com) — Fetches current weather and forecast for your selected coordinates.
  • Open-Meteo Air Quality API (air-quality-api.open-meteo.com) — Fetches air-quality, UV, and pollen data for your selected coordinates.
  • Open-Meteo Geocoding API (geocoding-api.open-meteo.com) — Converts a city search query into coordinates when you use manual location search.
  • National Weather Service (api.weather.gov) — Fetches US active weather alerts for your selected coordinates.
  • Nager.Date (date.nager.at) — Fetches public-holiday dates for your selected country when holiday auto-skip is enabled.
  • Windy (embed.windy.com and windy.com) — Displays the optional live radar map centered on your selected weather location. Windy's terms and privacy policy apply inside the embedded or external map.
  • RSS/news feeds — Fetches headlines from your selected public feed, such as Google News, BBC, NPR, Hacker News, or a custom URL.
  • User-configured webhooks — Sends alarm event payloads to the URL you configure, if you enable webhooks. Payloads can include event type, alarm identifier, alarm label, and timestamps. Use HTTPS and only endpoints you trust.
  • Internet radio streams — Streams audio from the station URL you configure.
  • Philips Hue bridge — Sends lighting commands to the bridge IP address and API key you configure on your local network.
  • YouTube alarm sounds, Play flavor only — Searches, previews, resolves, and downloads audio using NewPipe Extractor, yt-dlp, and direct media stream requests. F-Droid builds exclude this feature.

Core alarms, timers, stopwatch, local challenges, and local settings work without network access.

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device:

  • Alarms, settings, and alarm history are stored in a local SQLite database
  • Preferences are stored in Android DataStore / app-private local storage
  • Crash logs are written to app-private local files and are not uploaded automatically
  • Health Connect sleep sessions are read on demand for local Bedtime and Statistics summaries only. Sleep-session records are not copied into the app database or uploaded to the developer.
  • Smart-alarm actigraphy stores compact motion-bucket summaries locally for up to 30 days. The app does not retain raw accelerometer samples, and these buckets are not medical sleep-stage records.
  • Support bundles are created only when you choose to export one. They can include redacted wake-readiness diagnostics, aggregate smart-wake counts and decision reasons, recent alarm incident event codes, and local crash logs if present.
  • Downloaded alarm sounds are stored in device media storage when you choose to save them
  • Backup/restore exports data to a JSON file at a location you choose. Plain JSON backups may include alarms, labels, schedules, settings, integration URLs, Hue bridge details, webhook URLs, and other configuration values. Encrypted backups use your passphrase.
  • Shareable alarm links are created only when you choose to share an alarm.

Local Actigraphy Buckets

When smart-alarm monitoring is enabled, the app can summarize phone motion during the smart wake window into local awake-motion, light-motion, and still-motion buckets. These summaries are stored in the app database for up to 30 days so Statistics can explain recent smart-wake behavior. Raw accelerometer samples are not retained, the summaries are not exported in normal backups, and they are not uploaded to the developer.

Support Bundles

When you tap the support export action, the app creates a local ZIP file and hands it to Android's share sheet. The ZIP can include version/build/device details, wake-readiness status, aggregate smart-wake session counts and last decision reason, recent alarm incident event codes, redacted alarm diagnostics, and local crash logs if present. It omits alarm labels, custom media URIs, integration secrets, Health Connect records, and per-minute local actigraphy motion buckets. The app does not upload the ZIP automatically.

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. Optional features may contact the third-party services listed above. Those services receive the request data needed for the feature and are governed by their own policies.

Health Connect (Play flavor, opt-in)

The Play flavor can optionally connect to Android Health Connect after you turn on the Health Connect setting and grant sleep access. The app requests only android.permission.health.READ_SLEEP. It does not request write access, background health-data access, exercise routes, medical records, or other Health Connect data types.

  • Purpose: show recent sleep-session duration and stage summaries in Bedtime and Statistics.
  • Data read: recent Health Connect sleep sessions and sleep stages only.
  • Storage: summaries are used in memory for the current app UI. The app stores your Health Connect opt-in preference, but it does not copy sleep-session records into Room, DataStore, backups, crash logs, or a developer service.
  • Sharing: Health Connect data is not uploaded, sold, used for advertising, used for employment or insurance decisions, or shared with the developer.
  • Revocation: you can turn the app setting off or revoke access in Android Health Connect settings at any time.
  • F-Droid: the F-Droid flavor does not include the Health Connect SDK or request Health Connect permissions.

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The developer does not collect information from anyone through the app.
